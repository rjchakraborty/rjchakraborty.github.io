Private macOS utility

RJ Backup Automation

RJ Backup Automation is a private, single-user tool operated by Raja Chakraborty. It creates encrypted backups of selected local development projects and uploads the resulting archives to the owner's Google Drive account.

Not a public service

The utility has no public registration, hosted application server, advertising, analytics, or third-party users. It runs only on the owner's Mac.

How Google Drive access is used

  • Upload encrypted backup archives and checksum files.
  • Read file metadata and checksums to verify successful uploads.
  • Remove only older automation-managed backups after a replacement is verified.

Source folders remain local and are never modified by the backup process. Backup archives are encrypted locally before they are transferred to Google Drive.
